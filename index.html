    HOW TO PLAY
    • Every run starts with a 3·2·1 countdown — a whistle means GO. You can pre-hold during the countdown!
    • Keys: Space / ↑ press · P pause · M mute · Esc exit immersive.
    • CAVE — the classic ribbon. Hold to rise, release to fall. Thread the cave as it tightens.
    • FLAPPY — tap to flap through the pipes.
    • HELI — hold for thrust, release to drop. Dodge the blocks — and the birds!
    • SHIP — drag to steer; cannons fire on their own.
    • Crack glowing energy rocks for cores: faster bullets, 3 cores → GUN LV2, 6 → LV3.
    • Drones shoot back! Miss too many rocks and a BOSS warps in — deadlier at high scores. Sink it: +500, 3 bonus cores, and the heat turns up.
    • ⛶ Immersive — pure-black OLED mode. Pick your ink color in SETTINGS → IMMERSIVE INK.
    • Boss fights come with their own soundtrack. Good luck, pilot.
